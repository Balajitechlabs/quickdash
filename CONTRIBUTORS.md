## 🤝 Becoming a Contributor

We welcome community contributions from everyone! Whether it's:
* Adding or polishing translations in your native language
* Submitting bug fixes or performance optimizations
* Improving documentation, guides, and architectural specs
* Reporting edge cases and UI improvements

Please check out our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [Architecture Guide](ARCHITECTURE.md) to get started.
