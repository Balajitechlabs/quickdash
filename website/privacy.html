Privacy Policy & Data Safety Declaration

Application: QuickDash (com.balajitechlabs.quickdash)  ·  Effective Date: July 25, 2026

info Executive Summary

QuickDash is built with privacy as a foundational principle. The application operates 100% on-device without collecting, storing, or transmitting any personal data, usage metrics, device identifiers, or telemetry to external servers. No user registration is required, and no remote databases are used.

database 1. Data Collection & Transmission

We do not collect any Personal Identifiable Information (PII), such as your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or device ID.

  • No Remote Analytics: We do not integrate third-party tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Google Analytics).
  • No Crash Reporting Telemetry: Crash logs are kept strictly local to your Android system logcat and are never uploaded automatically.
  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register or sign in to use any feature of QuickDash.

key 2. Android Permissions Usage

QuickDash requests only the minimum Android framework permissions necessary to deliver its core floating utility functionalities:

• SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Display over other apps) Purpose: Allows the floating bubble widget and utility bottom sheet to render over other applications for seamless multitasking.
• CAMERA (QR Code Scanning) Purpose: Used exclusively when you explicitly tap to scan a Wi-Fi or UPI payment QR code. Camera frames are processed in real-time in memory via Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning API and are never recorded, saved, or transmitted.
• FOREGROUND_SERVICE (Quick Capture / Overlay Service) Purpose: Ensures the floating overlay service remains active without being killed by Android low-memory management while you interact with tools.

lock 3. Local Data Storage & Encryption

Data generated while using features (such as Quick Notes, Smart Clipboard history, and custom payment QR presets) is stored locally on your device using Android Room SQLite Database and EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256 encryption backed by Android Keystore).

child_care 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

QuickDash does not knowingly collect data from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no personal data from any user regardless of age, it fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

delete_sweep 5. User Data Deletion Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your physical device, you have full control over data deletion at any time:

  • Clear App Data: Go to Android Settings → Apps → QuickDash → Storage & Cache → Clear Data.
  • Uninstall App: Uninstalling QuickDash automatically purges all locally stored notes, clipboard entries, and settings.
