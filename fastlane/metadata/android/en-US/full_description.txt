<p>QuickDash is an open-source, system-wide floating productivity overlay for Android.</p>

<p><b>Key Features:</b></p>
<ul>
  <li><b>Customizable Radial Bubble Wheel:</b> Long-press or flick in any direction to access your favorite 4 shortcuts with RenderThread spring physics.</li>
  <li><b>1-Tap Control Center Quick Tile &amp; Glance Widgets:</b> Toggle the floating bubble or launch tools instantly with Material You dynamic color theming.</li>
  <li><b>12 Built-in Floating Tools:</b> Floating Calculator, Quick Notes, Offline Translator, OCR Text Extractor, Wi-Fi QR Share, Stopwatch, and universal UPI &amp; PayPal payment QR collector.</li>
  <li><b>100% On-Device Offline Privacy:</b> No accounts, no servers, zero telemetry.</li>
  <li><b>AES-256-GCM Encrypted Local Backups:</b> Export and restore your notes and settings securely (.qdbackup).</li>
</ul>
