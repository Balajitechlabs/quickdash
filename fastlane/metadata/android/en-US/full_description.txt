QuickDash is an open-source, system-wide floating productivity overlay for Android.

Key Features:
- 120Hz Customizable Radial Bubble Wheel: Long-press (350ms) or flick in any direction to access your favorite 4 shortcuts with RenderThread spring physics.
- 1-Tap Control Center Quick Tile & Glance Home Screen Widgets: Toggle the floating bubble or launch tools instantly with Material You dynamic color theming.
- 12 Built-in Floating Tools: Floating Calculator, Quick Notes, Offline Translator, OCR Text Extractor, Wi-Fi QR Share, Stopwatch, and universal UPI & PayPal payment QR collector.
- 100% On-Device Offline Privacy: No accounts, no servers, zero telemetry.
- AES-256-GCM Encrypted Local Backups: Export and restore your notes and settings securely.
