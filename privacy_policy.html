Privacy Policy for QuickDash

Effective Date: July 24, 2026

App Name: QuickDash

Developer: BalajiTechLabs

1. Overview

QuickDash respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal data. QuickDash operates primarily on-device, ensuring that your data remains private and securely stored on your local device.

2. Data Collection & Usage

We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

3. Permissions & On-Device Processing

  • Camera Permission: Used exclusively for scanning payment QR codes on-device. Camera streams are not recorded or uploaded anywhere.
  • Media Projection / Screen Recording: Used strictly when you explicitly initiate screen recording or doodle annotations. Recorded videos are saved locally to your device storage.
  • System Alert Window (Overlay Permission): Used to display floating quick-access tools over other applications.
  • On-Device Local AI & Translation: On-device AI models run 100% locally on your phone using MediaPipe GenAI. No prompt data or text processed by Local AI leaves your device.
  • Clipboard Access: Smart Clipboard history is stored securely in your encrypted local app storage. You can clear clipboard history at any time.

4. Data Safety Summary

  • Data Sold: No data sold to third parties.
  • Data Shared: No data shared with external marketing or tracking services.
  • Data Encrypted in Transit: HTTPS encrypted connections used exclusively for optional Cloud AI or optional currency conversion queries.

5. User Rights & Data Erasure

You have total control over your data. All saved notes, payment QR history, and clipboard history can be erased instantly inside the app settings by tapping "Clear History" or uninstalling the application.
